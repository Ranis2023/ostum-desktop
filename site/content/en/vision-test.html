Try the same question twice

  1. Open OSTUM Desktop with Zarum 9B and its vision projector installed. Basic chat is sufficient for this test. For a disconnected PC, prepare the model files in advance. Prepare an offline kit.
  2. Start a conversation. Click Рассмотреть фото (inspect a photo) or drop note-a.png into the chat. Paste the Russian question below and send it with the picture.
  3. Click Новый чат (new chat). Send note-b.png with the identical question. Compare the two numbers in each response with its image. The model may phrase an answer differently on your machine.
Прочитай записку на приложенном изображении. Во сколько встреча и какой номер кабинета? Ответь одной короткой фразой.

Both images use the same typeface. To evaluate handwriting or scanned forms, repeat the test with a sample from that workflow. Benchmark your own hardware.

Where the images were processed

Both the model and vision projector were read from disk. Web search was turned off. The local llama.cpp process used --offline and listened on 127.0.0.1. The laptop itself stayed online.
